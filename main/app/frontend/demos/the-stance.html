The position

A system that watches a room owes it a clear account of what it will not do.

These are not caveats buried in a policy. They are the shape of the product, stated before the features.

01

Not proctoring.

Chronos does not lock a browser, block apps, or produce a cheating verdict. Strict mode notices tab focus; it does not police it. The teacher decides what a flag means.

02

Not emotion recognition.

Gaze, head pose and eye openness are geometry. The system does not name a feeling, a mood or a diagnosis. A face is not a state of mind.

03

Not attendance.

A face in frame is not a register. Presence is a signal for the session, not an official record. NO_DATA is a camera miss, not an absence.

04

No automated discipline.

No score changes a grade. No flag removes a student. The student gets the first move and time to recover. The teacher has the last word, every time.

What we do instead

Point at a person. Leave the decision to a person.

One state change starts a short, visible chain. The student is nudged first; the teacher is never bypassed.

01

A state changes.

Attentive, distracted, away, disengaged, identity check or NO_DATA.

02

The student is nudged.

A private message and time to recover. No public callout.

03

Time to return.

The student gets the first move before the teacher escalates.

04

A liveness action, if needed.

Turn your head, smile — a 3–5 second window.

05

The teacher decides.

Mark engaged, message, ignore or remove. Nothing is automated.

“The signal points. The teacher decides.”
The record

One class. One page. No recording.

TPI is 60% mean engagement plus 40% attention retention, computed after class. It is a session index for the teacher — not a grade, not a ranking.

The evidence is small enough to read.

One integer, one timeline, one distribution, the students who need a follow-up, and the note the teacher wrote. That is the whole record.
