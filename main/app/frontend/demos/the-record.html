Six things, and nothing else.

The record is deliberately narrow. It answers what happened in one session and who may need a human follow-up. It does not score students, rank them, or decide anything.

No.PartWhat it tells you
01Teacher Performance IndexOne integer from 0–100, computed after class: 60% mean engagement, 40% attention retention.
02Engagement timelineThe session's shape, including the drift and the return. Snapshots, not a frame-by-frame replay.
03Attention distributionHow many learners sat in each band: attentive above 0.70, distracted 0.40–0.70, away below 0.40.
04Students at riskNames, average engagement, strikes, identity flags and the reason each learner was listed.
05Confusion hotspotsMoments when at least 20% of the class showed confusion at once. A prompt for the next class, not a verdict.
06ExportThe same page as a PDF. No hidden appendix, no second score.

Written by the room, signed by you.

Inference happens on the student's device. The teacher sees a state and chooses what to do. The record is assembled after the class from those choices and the session's analytics.

01 / IN THE BROWSER

Landmarks become a state.

FaceMesh reads gaze, head pose and eye openness. A face embedding checks identity consistency. No frame leaves the device.

02 / IN THE ROOM

The student gets first say.

A nudge goes to the student, with time to recover. Only repeated concerns reach the teacher. No face in frame is NO_DATA, not disengagement.

03 / AFTER CLASS

You write the last word.

Mark engaged, message, ignore or remove. The record reflects your decisions, then exports as a PDF for your own review.

The rule

Student first, teacher last word. Nothing is automated.

What the page refuses to hold.

Privacy is not a badge on this page. It is the shape of the record: a small set of signals, stored on purpose, with the raw material discarded.

Stored

  • A face embedding, so a returning student can be checked for consistency.
  • Session analytics: scores over time, states, strikes, flags and the final report.
  • The teacher's actions: nudges, messages, marks and removals.

Never stored

  • Video frames, with or without a face in them.
  • Page content, app content or the student's screen.
  • Emotion labels, grades, rankings or an automated decision about a student.
No Past Sessions is a shipped state. A first-time teacher has no history. The archive says so plainly, then gets out of the way.
