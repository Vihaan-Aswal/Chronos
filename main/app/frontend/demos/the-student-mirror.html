What is stored

Stored

  • A face embedding — a numerical signature, not a photo.
  • Session analytics: states over time and the final record.

Never stored

  • Video frames, with or without a face.
  • Page content, app content or your screen.
  • Emotion labels, grades or an automated decision.

The mirror shows geometry. It does not name a feeling, a mood or a diagnosis.

What the mirror does not show.

Not a mood

Gaze, head pose and eye openness are geometry. The mirror does not name a feeling or a diagnosis.

Not a grade

No student score decides anything. TPI is a session-level index for the teacher, computed after class.

Not a face on file

One embedding checks consistency. The frames are read in memory and never stored.
