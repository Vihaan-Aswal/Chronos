Design exploration / not a shipping build

Ten ways to introduce the same room.

Each direction is a complete homepage. They differ in what they prove first, what owns the first viewport, and the world they build — not just in colour.

Open them at 1440, 768 and 390. Every demo works with no backend and no account. Sample sessions are illustrative.

What the current home gets right

  • A coherent parchment/midnight palette and a real brand mark.
  • Privacy is present as an argument, not an afterthought.
  • “Signals, not surveillance” and “teacher judgment stays central” are the right sentences.

What it doesn't do

  • The hero shows a fake dashboard with a live “84.2% TPI” — TPI is a post-class integer, not a live metric.
  • It claims explainable states, nudges and identity but never shows one real state, nudge or record.
  • Only “Sign up” exists. A student with an invite link has no door; neither role is named.
  • At 390 the header's Sign in / Sign up pair crowds and wraps; the hero preview's bars and alert are blank on first paint.

What every direction keeps

  • Two roles, one room keyed by the session link.
  • Inference in the student's browser; embedding and analytics stored, frames and page content never.
  • Student first, teacher last word; nothing automated.
  • Both states of the lead: before the first class and after a completed one.

The ten directions.

Argument · lead · world · trade-off
01 / THE RECORD

The Record

The proof is the artifact: a completed session record with receipts.

Lead
A real session dossier — TPI, timeline, distribution, at-risk learners, PDF — with a first-class empty page one click away.
World
Registry ledger: cool grey paper, ink, oxide red, hard rules, tabular numerals, Manrope-led.
Optimizes
Concreteness and post-class trust. A skeptical teacher can see exactly what they get.
Trades
Immediacy. It sells the end of the class before the beginning; a teacher who wants to start now sees the archive first.
Open The Record
02 / THE INSTRUMENT

The Instrument

You can operate it: one student drifts, you nudge, they return, you end the class.

Lead
A working 20-learner console in the hero. Start a class, select a student, nudge, mark engaged, message, ignore, remove, end.
World
Night desk: near-black blue, phosphor mint, amber, thin chrome, dense Manrope, tabular data.
Optimizes
Product proof and teacher agency. It is impossible to leave without knowing what the tool does.
Trades
Warmth and student voice. A console-first page can still feel like supervision, even with the copy insisting otherwise.
Open The Instrument
03 / THE SIGNAL CHAIN

The Signal Chain

You can see exactly what crosses the device boundary, and what never does.

Lead
A drafting-sheet diagram: camera → landmarks → state → boundary → nudge → teacher → record, with stored/never-stored counters.
World
Blueprint: pale cyan ground, ink, cyan, oxide red, grid paper, title block, measured labels.
Optimizes
Privacy trust and mechanism clarity. The claim is locatable on the sheet, not just asserted.
Trades
Emotional pull. It is a diagram-first page for a skeptical buyer, not a student or a hurried teacher.
Open The Signal Chain
04 / ONE SESSION

One Session

One 42-minute class, told as a story: the drift, the nudge, the return, the note.

Lead
A seven-beat narrative with minute markers and marginalia: 00:00, 12:40, 13:10, 15:02, 26:10, 31:20, 42:00.
World
Annotated manuscript: chalk paper, oxblood, sage, wide margins, ruled lines, Newsreader-led.
Optimizes
Empathy and student-first judgment. It makes the human moment the product.
Trades
Feature completeness. A buyer sees one story, not the whole system; the console and the archive are implied.
Open One Session
05 / TWO DOORS

Two Doors

Two roles, one room: choose your door and see your version of the product.

Lead
A full-viewport split threshold. Teacher side and student side each carry their own empty/after state and CTA.
World
Threshold: deep teal, plaster, terracotta, brass, tall arches, warm light, Newsreader display.
Optimizes
Role clarity and emotional entry. Both users know which door is theirs in one second.
Trades
Depth. A chooser can feel like a fork before it feels like a product; the mechanism lives below the fold.
Open Two Doors
06 / THE READINESS CHECK

The Readiness Check

Trust is decided in the twenty seconds before class, not in a privacy badge.

Lead
A working readiness panel: camera, light, face, identity, consent, verify — first-time and returning states.
World
Daylight clinic: pale sage, white, deep green, amber, soft radii, practical Manrope, gentle status ticks.
Optimizes
First-run honesty, consent and recovery. Every failure has a name and a way out.
Trades
The classroom's live energy and the record's proof. It is a narrow moment, not the whole product.
Open The Readiness Check
07 / THE STANCE

The Stance

A position, stated plainly: what Chronos refuses to do — and what it does instead.

Lead
A full-width typographic statement that changes from the blank first class to one completed class, with a receipt of zeros or one record.
World
Broadside: black, bone, signal red, heavy Manrope, hard rules, no shadows, no decoration.
Optimizes
Trust through refusal. It says what the product will not do before it says what it does.
Trades
Proof by assertion. The least demonstrative direction; the mechanism lives in the receipt and the copy, not a live surface.
Open The Stance
08 / THE CLOCK

The Clock

Time is the unit; a session is only legible as 42 minutes.

Lead
A full-viewport 42-minute dial you scrub; the copy and the TPI resolve with the clock.
World
Swiss railway chronograph: bone, cobalt, signal red; Bricolage Grotesque + system mono; giant tabular numerals; no cards.
Optimizes
A live calculation and a time axis you can feel. The record only exists at 42:00.
Trades
The human story is compressed into the tape; a cold first impression.
Open The Clock
09 / THE CASE FILE

The Case File

Integrity is proved at the edges, not the average.

Lead
An openable exception dossier: NO_DATA, bad light, multi-face, identity mismatch.
World
Redacted forensic file: black, off-white, red; Bodoni Moda + system sans/mono; stamps, redaction bars.
Optimizes
The refusals as proof. Every edge case has a student view, a teacher view and a decision.
Trades
It is an exception file, not a normal class; everyday use is implied.
Open The Case File
10 / THE STUDENT MIRROR

The Student Mirror

The student is a person, not a row; show exactly what is measured and what is not.

Lead
A two-way mirror: the student's own signals, a switch to the teacher's view, a liveness action, a storage drawer.
World
Intimate optical instrument: cool silver-blue; Newsreader + Manrope (the one allowed); mirror frame; soft light.
Optimizes
Consent and legibility from the student's side; the teacher's view is one switch away.
Trades
One student's view, not the whole room; the class-level picture is elsewhere.
Open The Student Mirror
END / 10

That's ten.

Every direction keeps the same product truth: two roles, one room; inference in the student's browser; an embedding and session analytics stored, frames never; student first, teacher last word. The last three were built to break the first seven's section grammar: a clock, a file, a mirror.
