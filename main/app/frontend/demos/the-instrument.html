The states, exactly

Six labels. No blended score.

A student is in one state at a time. The bands are fixed, the flags are visible, and “no face in frame” has its own label. Nothing here is an emotion.

Away < 0.40 Distracted 0.40–0.70 Attentive > 0.70
AttentiveScore above 0.70. Still a signal, not a verdict.
Distracted0.40–0.70. The student gets time and a nudge first.
AwayBelow 0.40. The teacher sees the flag, never an automatic action.
Disengaged

Three strikes. The teacher can mark engaged, message, ignore or remove.

Identity mismatch

Two or more consecutive low-similarity checks, at a 2–5 minute cadence.

Multi-face

More than one face in frame. A flag for a human, not a cheating verdict.

Tab switch

Noticed only in strict mode. In normal mode, leaving the tab is not a violation.

No face in frame is NO_DATA.

It is not “disengaged.” A camera miss is a camera miss; the student is not punished for it.

Session controls

Strict or normal. Class or exam.

Two independent settings, both visible before class. Strict mode notices when the tab loses focus. Exam context applies a stricter gaze rule. Neither is a proctoring claim.

Tab policy

Normal: the student can switch tabs without it becoming a violation. Useful for classes that use other tools.

The setting is recorded with the session, so the report says which rule was in force.

Class context

Class: a glance away does not sink the score. The student has room to think, look at notes, or take a breath.

Exam: stricter gaze and one-strike disengagement. The teacher still makes every call.
After you end the class

The room becomes a record.

One integer, one timeline, one distribution. TPI is 60% mean engagement plus 40% attention retention, computed after class. It is a session index for the teacher, not a grade for the student.

You leave with the next class in mind.

The record names the students who need a follow-up and the moments the whole room stumbled. It does not rank anyone, and it does not decide anything.

  • Attention timeline and distribution, including NO_DATA gaps.
  • Students at risk with the reason: average, strikes or identity flags.
  • Confusion hotspots when at least 20% of the class was confused at once.
  • PDF export, including the strict/normal and class/exam settings.
