Three checks, no surprises

You can see every check.

Readiness is not a single opaque “verified” badge. Each check has a plain state, a reason and a way to recover. Nothing is hidden behind a spinner.

Camera

The browser asks for permission. A live preview shows what the model sees; the frame stays on the device.

Not: a recording. There is no server-side video archive.

Light

A brightness read of the frame. Too dark or too bright gets a plain instruction, not a failure.

Not: an image quality score. It only asks for enough light to read a face.

Face

FaceMesh finds a face and checks that it is centered. If it cannot, the state is NO_DATA — not “disengaged.”

Not: a mood, an emotion or a diagnosis. It reads geometry.

Identity

If you have enrolled, a face embedding is compared for consistency. Checks repeat every 2–5 minutes; a mismatch can ask for a liveness action in 3–5 seconds.

Not: a cheating verdict. The teacher sees a flag and decides.
No face in frame is NO_DATA.

A camera miss is a camera miss. It does not add a strike, and it does not call the student disengaged.

During class

What happens after you enter.

The check is not a one-time gate. Identity consistency is checked at a 2–5 minute cadence, and a nudge can arrive if the student drifts. The teacher still decides.

01

Join

The student opens the link and completes readiness. The teacher hosts the same room.

02

Read the room

FaceMesh reads gaze, head pose and eye openness on the device. The state changes.

03

Check identity

Every 2–5 minutes, a face embedding is compared. A mismatch can ask for a liveness action.

04

Nudge first

If a student drifts, a private nudge goes to them. They get time to recover before the teacher acts.

05

Teacher decides

Mark engaged, message, ignore or remove. The session record is written after class.

Normal tab: switching tabs is not a violation. Strict tab: losing focus is flagged; students are told before class. Class: a glance away does not sink the score. Exam: stricter gaze, one-strike disengagement.
What is stored

Small, specific, and visible.

“Nothing is stored” would be false. The honest version: a face embedding and the session's analytics are stored; the video frames and page content are not.

Stored

  • A face embedding — a numerical signature used to check consistency, not a photo.
  • Session analytics: scores over time, states, strikes, flags and the final report.
  • Teacher actions: nudges, messages, marks and removals.

Never stored

  • Video frames, with or without a face in them.
  • Page content, app content or the student's screen.
  • Emotion labels, grades, rankings or an automated decision about a student.
If something goes wrong

A way out, every time.

The check is allowed to fail. What matters is that the failure is named, explained and recoverable — without turning uncertainty into an accusation.

Camera denied

“Camera access is blocked.”

You see the browser instruction, not a dead end. The check cannot continue without a camera.

Fix: allow access in the browser, then retry. Or leave and join when you're ready.
No face found

“We can't see a face yet.”

The state is NO_DATA, not disengaged. No strike is added; the camera simply missed.

Fix: center your face and improve the light. Retry when you're ready.
Light is wrong

“It's too dark” or “too bright.”

A brightness read, not a judgment. The check tells you which way to adjust.

Fix: face a window or soften a lamp, then retry. The frame is not stored.
Identity mismatch

“Let's check it's still you.”

Two low-similarity checks can ask for a liveness action — turn your head, smile — in a 3–5 second window.

Fix: complete the action. The teacher sees a flag, not a verdict, and decides what happens next.
